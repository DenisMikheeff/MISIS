Question 7 · Improvement reserves

12–18 months: visibility → flow → economics

0–3 months

Measure

Map SCOR L2 processes; launch ABC by SKU/order/channel; baseline WAPE, OTIF, waste and cost-to-serve.

3–6 months

Synchronize

Supplier portal + CPFR for A-items; event forecasts; shared capacity and shelf-life alerts.

6–12 months

Optimize

Multi-echelon inventory, dynamic safety stock, route consolidation, slotting and cross-dock redesign.

12–18 months

Scale resilience

Dual sourcing for critical SKUs/lanes, 3PL scorecards, digital control tower and closed-loop waste program.
