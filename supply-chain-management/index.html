SCM Concepts
Chain Surplus
The objective of every supply chain
is to maximise total value generated.
Surplus = Value − SC Cost
Macro Processes
All SC activities within a firm
belong to SRM, ISCM, or CRM.
Integration is crucial for success.
Decision Phases
Design → Planning → Operation.
Each constrains the next and must
account for uncertainty over its horizon.
Push / Pull
Push: forecast-driven (Procurement,
Manufacturing). Pull: order-driven
(ВкусВилл customer order cycle).
Porter's View
Two Basic Types
According to M. Porter, every firm's
advantage is rooted in cost leadership
or meaningful differentiation.
Building Blocks
Efficiency, Quality, Innovation, and
Responsiveness to Customers are the
four pillars of competitive advantage.
Sustainable CA
A sustainable competitive advantage
must be Valuable, Rare, Inimitable,
and Non-substitutable (VRIN).
ВкусВилл's Edge
Private-label quality control, farm
partnerships, and a loyal app-based
customer base create differentiation.
